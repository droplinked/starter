## Overview

**Yield DROPS (DYLD)** represent the **junior tranche** in Droplinked's dual-token ecosystem,
acting as the **first-loss layer** and **primary credit enhancement** for Lend DROPS (DLND). By
absorbing initial losses and benefiting from residual platform fees, DYLD holders are
incentivized to maintain network health and sustainable growth.

DYLD serves multiple critical functions:

## Design philosophy

Yield DROPS represent the **upside** of the Droplinked ecosystem. DYLD entitles stakers to
**network fees** earned from real economic cash flow, and allows network growth to **compound
returns** for early adopters.

Broadly speaking, upside is the return paid for taking risk. Given the diversity of participants
in our ecosystem, early adopters of various types will be taking real risks through exposure to
Droplinked. In this way, DYLD can be viewed as an **incentive token**, offering network
participants upside in exchange for taking a risk on Droplinked.

In the early stage of Droplinked, we seek to incentivize network participation. Initially, DYLD
tokens will be allocated to network participants relative to their activity on the network.

While our initial framework for DYLD allocation is well optimized for an early-stage protocol,
we are confident that what Droplinked needs to incentivize for growth tomorrow will be different
than today. As a result, we have chosen a **protocol-wide risk-sharing mechanism**, a **dynamic
incentive policy**, and **macroprudential tools** to maximize the value of this real-yield
incentive token for all network participants while maintaining flexibility in an evolving and
competitive environment.

Given that DYLD is a token that provides access to real yield, upside will come in from both USD
stablecoin proceeds from network activity, and token value appreciation through projections of
network growth and yield distribution. As the network matures, outstanding DYLD volume will grow
along with token value. Yield, on a percentage basis, will decrease as a result of this
**inflationary pressure**.

Our final design element is aimed at increasing the **velocity of money** through the Droplinked
network while better aligning risk and reward. Specifically, while early adopters certainly take
meaningful risk in the early days, eventually network adoption leads to scale that de-risks
their participation. We expect the yield from network activity to pay multiples to the value
contributions of early adopters as a result of growth.

<Note>
  To ensure reward paid at any given time *t* is aligned with risk being taken by the network at
  least in the neighborhood of time *t*, Droplinked reserves the right to **dynamically burn
  older-vintage DYLD** — with some protections for foundational DYLD holders such as Droplinked
  itself, the team, early investors, and initial lenders, servicers, and origination partners.
</Note>

## Protocol-wide junior capital

DYLD utilizes a **global staking model** designed to efficiently mutualize risk across the
entire Droplinked lending platform.

### Global risk pooling

- DYLD staked tokens collectively form a diversified buffer against default risks across
  multiple lending vintages.
- Mitigates concentration risk, distributing losses broadly rather than isolating them to
  single pools.

### Stake lock-in

- DYLD tokens, once staked, **remain locked until network GMV milestones are reached**.
- Ensures stability and predictability for DLND investors by guaranteeing stable junior-capital
  protection.

### Capital adequacy ratio

- Droplinked maintains a targeted **DLND to staked DYLD capital adequacy ratio** (e.g., 9:1).
- The protocol monitors and manages DYLD staking incentives to ensure adequate capital support
  aligns with network growth.

## DYLD issuance

Initially, DYLD tokens will be allocated to network participants to incentivize constructive
behavior on the network.

| Participant | Primary issuance |
| --- | --- |
| **Merchants** | Issued primary-market DYLD **1:1** in exchange for contributing a % of sale proceeds to the Droplinked treasury **in excess of the 1% network fee** |
| **Affiliates** | Issued primary-market DYLD **1:1** in exchange for contributing a % of sales proceeds to the Droplinked treasury **in excess of the 1% network fee** |
| **Lenders** | Allocated **secondary-market** DYLD from the marketing & promotion budget early on to encourage capital formation |
| **Servicers** | Issued primary-market DYLD **inversely proportional to delinquent-loan experience** |
| **Underwriters** | Issued primary-market DYLD **inversely proportional to loan-loss experience** |
| **Investors** | Issued DYLD **1:1 with $ value paid in at TGE** |
| **Team, C-Corp, Marketing** | Issued at TGE |

<Note>
  Investor and Droplinked allocations, except the marketing allocation, are subject to a
  **6-month lock-up** for a **12-month vest**.
</Note>

## Yield distribution: GMV milestones

DYLD yield distribution incorporates **cumulative Gross Merchandise Value (GMV) thresholds**,
synchronizing token release with tangible economic growth.

This creates performance-driven incentives for early participants and team members, linking
token liquidity directly to **successful platform scaling**.

## Network fee sources

DYLD holders, bearing higher risks, receive substantial compensation from diversified income
streams.

### Sources

- **Network fee** of 1% on GMV value of all goods and services sold across the network
- **Origination fees** from new loans — acts as insurance for lenders given DYLD
- **Loan pool management fee**
- **Early-exit fees** from DLND holders redeeming prematurely
- **Excess yield generation**
- **Strategic reserve capital release** from low default rates

### Cash-flow waterfall

<Steps>
  <Step title="DLND stakers of matured pools — default losses">
    Cover realized default losses on recovered loans first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="DLND stakers of matured pools — outstanding principal">
    Cover outstanding loan principal in matured pools awaiting recovery, by buying outstanding
    DLND.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Droplinked foundation">
    Strategic reserves and growth capital.
  </Step>
  <Step title="DYLD stakers">
    Network participation and risk sharing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Income distribution

- Regular distributions occur through **stablecoin payouts**.
- Ensures steady economic returns, blending income yield with token-appreciation opportunities.

<Note>
  **DIP parameters managed under this policy:** network fee on GMV sales, loan origination fee,
  loan pool management fee, early-exit fees for DLND holders.
</Note>

## Originator and servicer incentives

DYLD incentives extend to loan originators and servicers to maintain alignment across the
lending ecosystem.

### Skin-in-the-game requirement

- Originators and servicers required to **stake all DYLD earned initially**, aligning
  underwriting quality with direct economic exposure to defaults.
- Enhances lender confidence through demonstrable commitment from origination partners.

### Performance-based rewards

- Servicers and originators earn DYLD incentives for maintaining default and recovery rates
  within predefined performance thresholds.

## Primary exchange

All primary issuance of DYLD is initially **staked until the next GMV milestone**. If holders of
DYLD then unstake to sell their tokens, they must first sell through the **Droplinked primary
exchange**.

The Droplinked primary exchange offers whitelisted lenders (DLND holders) a **transferable
right of first refusal** as DYLD liquidity providers on the primary exchange.

This mechanism gives lenders an additional avenue of recourse, allowing them to **buy DYLD
tokens at a discount** before they are sold to secondary-market sources.

## Macroprudential dynamic burn policy

Droplinked implements **adaptive burn policies** for DYLD as a proactive macroprudential
management tool.

<Note>
  **Worked example:** if target loan-pool demand is **$100 million** for a given quarter, but
  staked DYLD only amounts to **$8 million** of USD stablecoin in the treasury to back that
  pool, Droplinked may announce a **partial burn** — beginning with a portion of unstaked DYLD
  tokens released during the oldest loan vintages. Simultaneously, Droplinked issues new DYLD
  at a discount to market through the primary portal to capitalize against the loan pool.
</Note>

Such a dynamic burn mechanism offers numerous unique incentives for DYLD holders, and
particularly early adopters, to **maintain their staking** of DYLD. Particularly given that DYLD
positions which are never unstaked are **immune to burning**. It also offers a DYLD deflationary
counterbalance to the inflation associated with persistent primary-market issuance of DYLD
through sales and lending.

These sophisticated mechanics position Droplinked as a **robust, self-regulating trade
ecosystem** — offering financing options, digital infrastructure, dynamic risk management, and
global incentive alignment to sustain long-term stability and growth.
