## Historical DYLD burn priority

Under the macroprudential [dynamic burn policy](/guides/tokenomics/yield-drops#macroprudential-dynamic-burn-policy),
DYLD is burned in priority order. The historical/default priority is:

## DYLD allocation parameters per pool

Initial vs. target levels for DYLD allocations to lenders, originators, and servicers per loan
pool.

| Parameter | Initial level | Target level |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Lender DYLD per pool | 0.40% | 0.00% |
| Originator DYLD per pool | 0.40% | 0.10% |
| Servicer DYLD per pool | 0.20% | 0.05% |

The trajectory from initial to target reflects the DIP design intent — incentives are weighted
more heavily during early-stage capital formation, then taper as the network matures and yield
streams stabilize.
