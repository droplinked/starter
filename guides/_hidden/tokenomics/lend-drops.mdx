## Overview

**Lend DROPS (DLND)** is the main vehicle for institutional lenders to participate in the
Droplinked ecosystem. The token functions much like a **senior secured note** in a structured
product, aiming to give lenders **attractive interest rates** with **strong principal
protection**.

DLND supports CDP-style lending against real-world assets (RWAs) with on-chain inventory records
as part of the collateral picture.

## Design philosophy

"Never lose money" is an unspoken rule of institutional finance, particularly the debt investors
that find this product attractive. DLND is designed to ensure Droplinked lenders have:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Loan pool vintaging">
    Clear delineation of asset risk and return through loan-pool **vintaging**, mimicking
    traditional finance securitization managers and their associated shelves.
  </Step>
  <Step title="1:1 principal representation">
    Direct representation of outstanding principal through the **1:1 relationship** of USD
    stablecoin paid-in vs. DLND issued.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Credit enhancement via DYLD staking">
    Principal protection from DYLD as junior risk-buffer capital.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Originator and servicer alignment">
    DYLD reward pool tied to default experience — originators and servicers earn (or lose)
    rewards based on loan quality.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Novel recourse mechanism">
    Exclusive primary-exchange LP rights for DYLD — another avenue of recourse for DLND
    holders.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Legal enforceability">
    Smart-contract **mirroring** of papered legal contracts between lenders, SPVs, dLend, and
    origination/servicing partners.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The legal contract layer is mirrored on-chain between:

- Lenders, SPVs, and **dLend** — the Droplinked loan manager
- dLend and borrowers
- dLend and origination partners
- dLend and servicing partners

## First loan pool

The first loan pool and subsequent pools may remain **private** to ensure safe and stable
development of business and legal terms that are suitable for smart-contract mirroring. dLend's
first priority is securing lenders, with second priority to Droplinked's mission.

Initially, dLend and partners will target **established, growing businesses** seeking
**inventory financing** and **MRR/ARR-based working capital loans** in jurisdictions that
overlap with our origination and servicing partners to ensure high quality loan origination,
contract enforceability, and incentive alignment.

<Note>
  Loan pools will be domiciled in the **Caymans** to facilitate global lender aggregation,
  speed, cost efficiency, and a crypto-friendly environment, with potential feeder-fund launches
  down the road for expanded jurisdictional efficiency.
</Note>

## Subsequent loan pools

### Progressive digitalization

Subsequent loan pools will be **gradually brought on-chain** with increasing digital
functionality. This will allow Droplinked, dLend, and our lenders, originators, servicing, and
origination partners to collaboratively develop an integrated digital infrastructure that solves
meaningful operational pain points for each partner.

By building and designing the digital infrastructure with our partners, we hope to create unique
synergies and meaningful cost efficiencies while offering our partners upside in the protocol's
success. Over time, we hope to leverage our experience, data collection, and AI to **fully
automate capital and resource allocation** from capital raise to principal repayment.

This approach also offers our traditional partners an opportunity to develop expertise in the
growing and evolving blockchain space.

### Progressive risk stratification

Financing activity will grow and evolve over time to include more financing types, a broader
borrower base, different maturity profiles, and different collateral types. As financing
activity expands, this will require increasing stratification to vintages to isolate specific
risk profiles for relevant investors seeking factor exposures.

In the long run, we envision a **Market Pool** where each dollar is allocated pro-rata to the
factor pools based on demand. We also envision **custom pools** where investors seeking a
specific mix of exposures may select a custom allocation to factor pools.

### Progressive decentralization

Accountability is implicit in the legal agreements to which institutional lenders are
accustomed. Ensuring accountability in today's world of centralized entities governing by law
requires centralized actors that can exercise lawful authority to ensure successful outcomes for
our lenders.

As our servicing, underwriting, and managing partners increasingly realize the upside benefits
of Droplinked network participation, we will progressively incentivize **on-chain transparency
and decentralization** as our partners' capabilities develop.

## Issuance through dLend Primary Portal

By centralizing issuance via the **dLend Primary Portal**, Droplinked ensures a **bond-like**
primary market environment, ideal for institutions that prefer a clear, regulated entry point
into the protocol.

### Institutional onboarding and compliance

Lenders must pass dLend due diligence including **KYC and AML** and execute legal agreements
standard for bankruptcy-remote SPVs. This process is part of the broader onboarding to the
Droplinked platform, providing whitelisted lenders with priority access to lending pools and
DYLD liquidity provisioning.

### Subscription window

Each pool will have a defined subscription window with **early DYLD incentives** to encourage
capital formation. For every $1 contributed, **1 DLND token** will be issued to digital wallets
initially abstracted to our lenders. This simplifies accounting and ensures clarity: **1 DLND =
$1 principal claim**.

### Smart-contract allocation

Dollars paid-in — either through traditional bank wire or through our crypto rails — will be
allocated DLND proportionally. Those dollars will be converted to USD stables and deposited into
the relevant loan pool's vault. Minted DLND is then immediately **staked** in the pool,
representing the lender's share in that vintage.

## Redemption and early exits

### Periodic amortization burns

As businesses repay principal and interest on loans, the underlying principal at risk will
return to the pool vault as USD stablecoin. On a pre-defined periodic basis (monthly for trade
finance loans), excess DLND reflecting the portion of repaid principal for the pool vintage will
be **auto-burned** — triggering the return of principal and interest to lenders in their
preferred format.

This process is akin to **structured-security repayment dynamics**, making it a familiar process
for institutional investors.

### Scheduled redemption

Each pool vintage will have a scheduled **maturity date**. Upon maturity, any remaining repaid
principal or interest will be distributed to DLND stakers.

### Delinquent loans

As loans become delinquent, loan servicers will work proactively with the borrowers, dLend, and
our managing partners to rapidly mitigate excess losses. **Loan modification**, **collateral
liquidation**, and **legal action** all offer means of recourse and loss mitigation for lenders.

### DYLD credit enhancement

<Note>
  The protocol targets a **10% credit enhancement** from DYLD stakers against the original
  amount of DLND issued. This credit enhancement is supported by USD stablecoins in the
  Droplinked treasury vault.
</Note>

At maturity, outstanding DLND, **up to 10%**, representing unpaid principal on delinquent loans
will be purchased by the Droplinked treasury from the proceeds generated by network fees and
Droplinked investor capital.

Any DLND outstanding **in excess of 10%** will be held as staked in the pool vault, representing
each investor's claim to recovery proceeds on delinquent loans. The dLend team will continue to
work with special servicers to maximize value for DYLD stakers and lenders.

## Liquidity management and early redemptions

DLND represents principal investment in real loans with real repayment timelines. Even so,
institutional investors seek assurance that liquidity mechanisms and transferability of their
loan interest exist.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Secondary market & bi-lateral" icon="arrow-right-arrow-left">
    Whitelisted investors can buy staked DLND from invested lenders, subject to legal
    documentation of the transactions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Exit queue" icon="list">
    Investors can request early redemption, placed in a redemption queue, and processed based
    on available liquidity.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Auction-based liquidity" icon="gavel">
    For investors seeking early redemption, Droplinked may run periodic staked-DLND auctions to
    aggregate supply and demand and clear at a fair price.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Fire-sale prevention" icon="shield">
    Through the DIP, Droplinked reserves the right to limit monthly outflow for live loan
    pools — protecting both Droplinked and at-risk lenders from impairments due to rapidly
    falling trade prices.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Strategic liquidity reserve" icon="vault">
    The Droplinked treasury maintains a strategic liquidity reserve for funding in
    emergencies — in-force staked DLND may be purchased depending on circumstances.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

This multifaceted approach mirrors **open-ended private debt funds** or **interval funds**,
which allow partial liquidity while safeguarding the overall loan pool integrity.

## DLND investor benefit: DYLD primary exchange LPs

Primary DYLD issuance occurs via buy-in from merchants and affiliates through their own election
of discretionary allocations as a percentage of GMV sales. It is also issued to servicers and
originators as an incentive for value maximization within loan pools.

Lenders will receive some DYLD from the marketing budget as an incentive for participation
initially. Over time, this marketing-budget incentive is replaced with a **right of first
refusal** on all primary-market DYLD sales — giving lenders another structural advantage on top
of senior-secured exposure.
