## Dual-token framework

Droplinked uses a **dual-token model** to track, incentivize, and reward constructive behavior
in the ecosystem, pairing **Lend DROPS (DLND)** as the senior claim on principal and interest
for on-chain financing with **Yield DROPS (DYLD)** as a junior risk-buffer. This structure
**mirrors** the senior/junior tranching found in the traditional securitization market, where
subordinated residual tranches absorb losses first — providing **principal protection** and
**credit enhancement** to DLND holders.

Both tokens serve multiple functions, offering thoughtful and highly aligned incentives rooted
in the financial primitives of borrow/lend, time value of money, pooled risk, diversification,
and economic fairness. These incentives provide a robust foundation for a growth-focused stable
network, and a familiar interface for TradFi natives seeking digital yields.

## Design philosophy

The Droplinked tokenomics design is built **from first principles**, with a foundation in
traditional finance primitives — modeled after structures commonly recognized across public and
private fixed-income and equity markets, and more specifically, **asset-backed securities
(ABS)**.

The design also incorporates biomimetic inspiration through unique feedback loops, stakeholder
gateway access and control, and the **Dynamic Incentive Policy**. Additionally, Droplinked
embeds a number of macroprudential tools and options. These innovations help create a novel,
robust, and anti-fragile network through incentive alignment.

## Dynamic Incentive Policy (DIP)

Droplinked has developed a **Dynamic Incentive Policy (DIP)**. Acknowledging that our product
exists in the midst of an accelerating technological revolution, we believe fixed incentive
policies should not be applied to the fluidity of global commerce.

Through the DIP, the Droplinked foundation may tactically and strategically fine-tune network
incentives to ensure network stability and fairness for all. The DIP also provides a means for
**gradual decentralization** of the protocol as the network matures, incentives stabilize
toward long-run averages, and decentralized governance develops.

## Lend DROPS (DLND)

Lend DROPS provide a **stable yield** with **strong principal protection** for lenders,
grounded in real economic cash flow driven by global trade. It also provides a robust capital
foundation for Droplinked merchants, creating unique ecosystem efficiency for loan operations
with on-chain inventory records and smart contract automated revenue distribution and
settlement.

Lend DROPS are issued **1:1 with USD stablecoin value** deposited into lending pools to ensure
Lend DROPS maintain a real, practical tie to the value of outstanding loan principal.

See [Lend DROPS (DLND)](/guides/tokenomics/lend-drops) for the full mechanism.

## Yield DROPS (DYLD)

Yield DROPS provide strong **credit enhancement** for DLND holders, as DYLD is staked for
network yield against protocol-wide diversified risk exposures. The network yield is generated
from small rates charged across the network for various types of activity and collected into the
**Droplinked Treasury**. These accumulated network fees are distributed upon GMV sales
milestones, providing novel incentives for network growth from all participants.

Upon reaching these milestones, portions of network yield will be distributed as follows:

<Steps>
  <Step title="DLND stakers of matured pools">
    First, to DLND stakers of matured lending pools to cover default losses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Droplinked foundation">
    Then, to the Droplinked foundation for strategic liquidity reserves and growth capital.
  </Step>
  <Step title="DYLD stakers">
    Finally, to DYLD stakers for network participation and risk sharing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Emissions

  This section is a work in progress and may change as the protocol matures.
</Note>

Aside from the pre-allocated supply tokens, the only way for new DROPS tokens to be generated is
through **sales activity related to smart product contract (SPC) listings**. These new tokens
are allocated to contributors along the commerce supply chain, with pre-defined splits defined
by the producer. Tokens are only distributed once a sale is recorded and settled. As such, the
aggregate amount of tokens paid to producers and publishers is variable, leading to a variable
total supply based upon network activity.

### Pre-allocated supply

| Allocation | Amount (DROPS) |
| --- | ---: |
| Investors | 25,000,000 |
| Team & development | 15,000,000 |
| C-Corp allocation | 10,000,000 |
| Marketing & promotions | 10,000,000 |

Token distribution is released over an **18-month period** with a **6-month cliff** from TGE.

## Network milestones

DYLD payouts occur when the network hits **GMV sales milestones**. As DROPS tokens are issued in
proportion to total network sales, outstanding DROPS at each milestone can be estimated, along
with the estimated annualized yield for stakers. In the modeling, ~55M DROPS tokens are assumed
to be unlocked to investors, team, etc. between Phase 2 and Phase 4.

## Framework and definitions

### Droplinked SPC pool

This allocation is reserved for the decentralized registration of products linked to smart
contracts. When a manufacturer or producer lists an item through Droplinked's decentralized
inventory management system, they define their payout amounts along with the % or commission fee
paid to a publisher or promoter for generating the sale. By default, every conversion
contributes **1% of each sale as a network fee** back into the Droplinked treasury vault.

### Droplinked treasury vault

The tokenized yield stored into the Droplinked treasury will be converted to **USD stablecoins**
to maintain value while minimizing volatility. **Threshold milestones** trigger yield payouts
based on phases for the total amount of gross merchandise value sold across the network.
