A merchant's **KYB cohort** answers four questions at once:

1. **Who is the Merchant of Record (MoR)?** — the merchant themselves, the PSP, or Shopsadiq Ltd
2. **What KYB sources feed the merchant's compliance record?** — PSP OAuth, MoR master KYB, merchant-direct OAuth, or Sumsub fallback
3. **Where does settlement land?** — merchant's bank, PSP master, or Shopsadiq master
4. **Which lending tier can underwrite this merchant?** — CredibleX (Tier 1, UAE), US LFI corridor (Tier 2), Tier-3 vault (Q3-Q4 2026)

Routing decisions in the platform (PSP onboarding offer set, doc checklist, lending tier
selection, 1099-NEC export inclusion, FX reconciler attribution) all switch on the cohort.

## What the cohort decides

### MoR entity

| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `merchant` | Merchant is the regulated entity; PSP is gateway-only |
| `telr` / `paypal` / `paymob` / `bonum` | PSP carries MoR via its own aggregator product |
| `shopsadiq` | Shopsadiq Ltd is the MoR under its 2026-05-13 Telr partnership (and analogous future PSP partnerships) |

The MoR entity is who bears chargeback risk, who carries the consumer disclosure obligation,
and whose bank account settlement lands in. It also determines who is named in FTC + 1099-NEC
filings.

### KYB sources

A merchant's `KybRecord.sources[]` is a chain of KYB attestations the platform has inherited
or verified. The cohort decides which sources are required:

| Source | When |
|---|---|
| `MERCHANT_DIRECT_OAUTH` | Merchant connects their own PSP account via OAuth (Connect cohorts) |
| `PSP_OAUTH` | PSP OAuth from a master account (MoR-via-Shopsadiq cohorts use the Shopsadiq-side Telr OAuth) |
| `PSP_MOR` | Master KYB inherited from a MoR aggregator (Telr-Direct, PayPal-CP, Shopsadiq-master, etc.) |
| `SUMSUB` | Fallback when no PSP OAuth path is available — the merchant runs Sumsub directly |

### Lending-tier eligibility

The cohort gates which lending tiers can underwrite a merchant:

| Tier | Lender | Eligible cohorts |
|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1** | CredibleX (UAE), planned US LFI corridor | UAE-jurisdictioned cohorts (Telr-Connect, Telr-Direct, Shopsadiq-via-Telr, Paymob-Connect EG, Paymob-Direct) |
| **Tier 2** | US LFI corridor lenders (pilots Q3 2026) | US-jurisdictioned cohorts (Stripe-Connect, PayPal-Connect, future US Shopsadiq partnerships) |
| **Tier 3** | Droplinked-direct $100M FSRA vault (first loans Q4 2026) | All cohorts; tier gating happens at vault policy level instead of cohort level |

### Doc checklist depth

| Cohort family | Doc count | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| `A_MOR_SHOPSADIQ_VIA_*` | Light (3) | Sub-merchant inherits Shopsadiq master KYB; only identity + PSP linkage + recent settlement needed |
| `A_MOR_*_DIRECT` | Medium (4) | Sub-merchant inherits PSP master KYB; add MoR consent letter |
| `A_CONNECT_*` | Heavy (5) | Merchant is the regulated entity; full bank statement + tax/identity pack |

## Reading a merchant's cohort

```
GET /developers/kyb/v1/merchants/{merchant_id}
```

### curl

```bash
curl https://apiv3.droplinked.com/developers/kyb/v1/merchants/merch_2j8... \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer ds_test_REPLACE_ME" \
  -H "X-InventoryOS-Version: 2026-06-01"
```

### TypeScript

```ts
import { DroplinkedClient } from "@droplinked/sdk";

const droplinked = new DroplinkedClient({
  apiKey: process.env.DROPLINKED_API_KEY ?? "ds_test_REPLACE_ME",
});

const merchant = await droplinked.kyb.merchants.get("merch_2j8...");
console.log(merchant.cohort, merchant.mor_entity, merchant.kyb_sources);
```

## How cohort is assigned

Cohort is assigned at PSP-onboarding time:

1. Merchant signs up and picks a jurisdiction.
2. PSP options are filtered to the merchant's jurisdiction (e.g. GCC merchants see Telr in
   three modes: Connect / MoR-Direct / MoR-via-Shopsadiq).
3. Merchant picks the mode; the KYB orchestrator writes `cohort` to the `KybRecord` and
   inherits the appropriate KYB sources.
4. Subsequent lending applications, PSP wiring, and settlement routing all read this cohort.

A merchant can re-onboard into a different cohort (e.g. switch from `A_CONNECT_TELR` to
`A_MOR_SHOPSADIQ_VIA_TELR`) — this generates a new `KybRecord` revision; the old one is
preserved for audit.

## Why eleven cohorts (not three)

Telr has three distinct MoR cohort patterns per operator clarification — Connect (merchant
MoR), MoR-Direct (Telr is MoR via its aggregator), and MoR-via-Shopsadiq (Shopsadiq is MoR per
the 2026-05-13 partnership agreement). Bonum is analogous. Stripe, PayPal, and Paymob each
support Connect + MoR-Direct + (planned) MoR-via-Shopsadiq. The total cohort count expands as
PSP partnerships mature; the platform routes from `cohort` consistently so adding a new cohort
is additive to the matrix, not a refactor.
